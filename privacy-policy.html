Privacy Policy - Yamu

Effective Date: January 20, 2025

1. Introduction

Welcome to Yamu ("we," "our," or "us"). Yamu is a road trip planning and social networking mobile application that helps you plan trips with friends, discover destinations, and share travel experiences.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application Yamu (the "Service"). Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. If you do not agree with the terms of this Privacy Policy, please do not access the application.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Account Information

When you create an account with Yamu, we collect:

  • Email Address: Used for account creation and authentication
  • Phone Number: Required for account verification and friend discovery
  • Display Name: Your chosen username or display name
  • Profile Photo: Optional profile picture from Google Sign-In or manually uploaded
  • Authentication Data: Password or Google Sign-In credentials

2.2 Trip and Travel Data

  • Road Trip Information: Trip titles, destinations, dates, notes, and itineraries
  • Location Data: Destination coordinates, addresses, and location pins you share
  • Trip Participants: Information about friends you invite to trips
  • Messages and Discussions: Conversations and messages within trip discussions
  • Checklists and Activities: Items you add to trip checklists and activity plans

2.3 Social Features Data

  • Contact Information: Access to your device contacts (with permission) to find friends
  • Friendship Data: Friend connections, friend requests sent and received
  • Social Interactions: Messages, location shares, and collaborative trip planning activities
Important: We only access your contacts with explicit permission to help you find friends who are already using Yamu. Your contact list is not stored on our servers.

2.4 Device and Usage Information

  • Device Information: Device type, operating system version, device identifiers
  • Usage Analytics: How you interact with the app, features used, time spent
  • Performance Data: App crashes, errors, and performance metrics
  • Network Information: IP address, connectivity status for offline functionality

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • Account Management: Create and manage your Yamu account
  • Trip Planning: Enable collaborative road trip planning with friends
  • Social Features: Connect you with friends and enable social interactions
  • Friend Discovery: Help you find friends using phone number matching
  • Notifications: Send push notifications about trip updates, friend requests, and messages
  • App Improvement: Analyze usage patterns to enhance features and performance
  • Customer Support: Provide technical support and respond to inquiries
  • Safety and Security: Detect fraud, abuse, and ensure app security

4. Information Sharing and Disclosure

We respect your privacy and do not sell your personal information. We may share your information in the following limited circumstances:

4.1 With Other Users

  • Trip Participants: Information you share within trips is visible to all trip participants
  • Friends: Your profile information (name, photo) is visible to accepted friends
  • Shared Content: Messages, location pins, and notes shared in trips

4.2 Service Providers

  • Firebase/Google Cloud: For authentication, database, and cloud storage
  • Push Notification Services: For sending app notifications
  • Analytics Services: For app performance monitoring (anonymized data)

4.3 Legal Requirements

  • When required by law or court order
  • To protect our rights, property, or safety
  • To prevent fraud or illegal activities

5. Third-Party Services

Yamu integrates with the following third-party services:

  • Google Sign-In: For account authentication
  • Firebase: For authentication, database, and push notifications
  • Google Play Services: For various Google services integration

6. Data Security

We implement robust security measures to protect your information:

  • Encryption: Data is encrypted in transit and at rest
  • Firebase Security: Leveraging Google's enterprise-grade security infrastructure
  • Access Controls: Strict access controls and authentication requirements
  • Offline Protection: Secure offline data storage on your device

However, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Data Retention

We retain your information as follows:

  • Account Data: Until you delete your account
  • Trip Data: Indefinitely or until you delete specific trips
  • Messages: Stored with trip data for collaborative planning purposes
  • Analytics Data: Anonymized data may be retained for service improvement

8. Your Rights and Choices

8.1 Account Control

  • Profile Management: Update your profile information anytime
  • Trip Control: Create, edit, or delete your trips
  • Friend Management: Add or remove friends, manage friend requests
  • Account Deletion: Delete your account and associated data

8.2 Privacy Controls

  • Contact Access: Revoke contact permissions in device settings
  • Notifications: Control push notification preferences
  • Data Access: Request a copy of your personal data
  • Data Correction: Request correction of inaccurate information
To exercise these rights: Contact us at the information provided below, or use the in-app settings and account management features.

9. Children's Privacy

Yamu is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately.

10. Location Data

Yamu uses location data in the following ways:

  • Trip Destinations: Store coordinates of destinations you add to trips
  • Location Sharing: Share location pins with trip participants (only when you choose to)
  • No Background Tracking: We do not continuously track your location

All location sharing is explicit and controlled by you.

11. Offline Functionality

Yamu offers offline capabilities:

  • Trip data is cached locally for offline access
  • Changes sync automatically when you reconnect
  • Offline data is encrypted and secured on your device

12. International Data Transfers

Your information may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your own, including the United States where our Firebase infrastructure is located. We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place to protect your information during such transfers in accordance with applicable data protection laws.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. We will notify you of any material changes by:

  • Posting the new Privacy Policy in the app
  • Updating the "Effective Date" at the top of this policy
  • Sending notifications for significant changes

Your continued use of Yamu after any changes constitutes acceptance of the updated Privacy Policy.

14. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your data, please contact us:

  • Email: privacy@yamu.app
  • Support: support@yamu.app
  • Developer Contact: Available through app store listings

We will respond to privacy-related inquiries within 30 days.
